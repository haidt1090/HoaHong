# Phase 6 — Sprint 13: QR Code System

- **Phase:** 6 — QR Code & Online Results
- **Sprint:** 13
- **Priority:** 🟢 Medium
- **Django App(s):** `qrcodes`
- **Depends on:** Phase 1 (patients), Phase 2 (examinations)

---

## Goal

Generate encrypted QR codes for test tubes, booklets, and result lookup; support batch generation for printing; and enable QR scanning in the frontend to speed up data entry.

---

## Tasks

- [ ] `QRCode` model:
  - `patient`: FK → Patient
  - `examination`: FK → Examination (nullable)
  - `code`: CharField (encrypted unique identifier)
  - `type`: ChoiceField (`test_tube` / `booklet` / `result`)
  - `generated_at`: DateTimeField
  - `printed`: BooleanField
- [ ] QR generation API: `GET /api/qr/generate/?patient_id=X&type=test_tube` → PNG (or base64)
- [ ] QR content format (encrypted, not raw IDs):
  - Test tube: `https://ycaohoahong.com/khamsk/qr/test/<encrypted_id>`
  - Result lookup: `https://ycaohoahong.com/khamsk/qr/result/<encrypted_id>`
  - Booklet: `https://ycaohoahong.com/khamsk/qr/booklet/<encrypted_id>`
- [ ] Encryption for QR codes (AES or signed JWT, not raw IDs)
- [ ] Batch QR generation: `POST /api/qr/batch/`
  - Input: list of `patient_ids` + `exam_type`
  - Output: ZIP of QR images or a PDF with labels
- [ ] QR scanning (frontend via `html5-qrcode`):
  - Camera → scan → auto-fill patient for examination entry
  - Scan test tube QR → show patient + pending lab tests
  - Scan result QR → redirect to result portal
- [ ] Print QR on labels (CSS label layout: patient name, exam date, QR)

---

## Endpoints

| Method | Path | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| GET | `/api/qr/generate/` | Single QR (query: patient_id, type) |
| POST | `/api/qr/batch/` | Batch generate (ZIP/PDF of labels) |
| GET | `/api/qr/scan/{code}/` | Decode QR → patient/exam info |

---

## Security

- QR content encodes an **encrypted** token, never a raw DB id.
- Decoding validates + decrypts server-side; invalid tokens rejected.
- Result-lookup QR still requires the portal password step (Sprint 14).

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- Generating a QR returns a scannable PNG whose payload decrypts to the correct record
- Batch generation produces printable labels for a list of patients
- Scanning a test-tube QR in the frontend loads the correct patient for entry
- Encrypted codes cannot be guessed/enumerated to reveal other patients
- QR labels print with patient name + exam date + code

---

## Technical Notes

- Use `qrcode` (Python) for generation; `html5-qrcode` (JS) for scanning.
- Store `code` as the encrypted string (unique); keep a stable mapping to `patient`/`examination`.
- Permission (per matrix): QR generate = Admin/Doctor/LabTech; scan-for-entry = Admin/Doctor/Nurse/LabTech.
- These QR codes feed the booklet cover page slot from Sprint 10.
